0.3.0

- Requires PyJWT[crypto]>=2.15, whose key cache survives a failed refresh.
- Keycloak authentication verifies tokens against the realm's published signing keys,
  and checks expiry and issuer (and audience, when keycloak.resource is set).
  Requires keycloak.issuer or multiauth.policy.keycloak.issuer.
- keycloak.algorithms lists the realm's signing algorithms (default RS256);
  shared-secret and unsigned algorithms are refused.
- The realm's keys are re-fetched every 5 minutes, so removed keys stop being
  trusted; a token naming an unknown key re-fetches them at most once a minute.
- The shared-secret JWT policy verifies token signatures. With an asymmetric
  algorithm and only jwt.private_key set, tokens are verified with its public key.
